Notes

Wi-Fi uses the out-of-tree b43-next AC-PHY driver. Settings live in /etc/router-wifi.conf; apply them with /etc/init.d/router-wifi restart. Radios stay off until wifi-radio on, so nothing is transmitted by default.

Interrupt affinity is set by /etc/init.d/router-irq: Ethernet and the 2.4 GHz radio on CPU1, the 5 GHz radio on CPU0. Without it both radios and Ethernet share CPU0 and the 2.4 GHz receive ring can overflow under 5 GHz load.

LAN address and recovery differ per board; the router project page shows this unit's address, MAC/CFE notes and the serial + TFTP recovery steps.
